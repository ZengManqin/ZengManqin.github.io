  * **Research Area:** Tactile sensing technology and multimodal fusion.
  * **Bio-inspired Event-Frame Dual-Modal Tactile Sensor:** Engineered a bio-inspired optical tactile sensor utilizing a DAVIS346 camera to replicate the human fast-slow tactile pathways, achieving microsecond-resolution dynamic event streams and grayscale frames with precise spatio-temporal alignment.
