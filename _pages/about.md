Welcome to my personal page! I am a Joint Master's student at the **Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech)** and the **Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology (SIAT), CAS**, co-advised by Prof. Xinyu Wu (IEEE Fellow) and Prof. Zhengkun Yi. Additionally, I work as a Research Assistant at **The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)** under the supervision of Prof. Li Zhang (IEEE Fellow).

🔥 **I am actively seeking PhD opportunities starting in Fall 2027!** 

My research focuses on bridging the gap between novel hardware design and AI-driven data processing. I am particularly interested in wrist-fingertip collaborative tactile perception, multimodal fusion networks, and soft robotic 3D printing.

📍 **Upcoming:** I will be attending **IROS 2026** in the US this September for an **Oral Presentation** on our bio-inspired event-frame dual-modal tactile sensor. I would love to connect and chat if you are also attending!

## Research Interests
* **Tactile Sensing & Multimodal Learning:** Event-frame dual-modal sensors, data fusion strategies, and state recognition networks for robotic assembly.
* **Robotic Mechanism & Hardware Design:** Mechanical design, CAD modeling, and physical prototyping of novel sensors and robotic structures.
* **3D Printing for Soft & Biomaterials:** Additive manufacturing strategies for unconventional materials and extrusion nozzle design.
